# Acceptance evidence

Written reports, measurements and checksums are source-controlled. Screenshots
and videos are local artifacts and are excluded from Git, including historical
captures removed in the owner's 2026-11-01 history cleanup.

Image links in the historical reports work when those archived captures are
present locally. They are not included in a fresh source-only clone. Original
captures and original revision IDs are preserved in the local history bundle;
see [archive and recovery](../handoffs/67-source-only-history.md). Re-running a
capture produces new evidence, not a replacement for the original observation.
